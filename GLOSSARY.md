# Poopable

Poopable helps anyone quickly find a suitable nearby restroom they can use now, supported by ratings, reviews, and recommendations.

## Language

**Restroom**:
A particular toilet facility with its own entrance, distinct from the business or building containing it. Separately entered men's, women's, and all-gender facilities, including facilities on different floors, are separate restrooms; stalls within a shared facility belong to that restroom.
_Avoid_: Bathroom, venue (when referring to the facility)

**Restroom listing**:
An entry describing one particular restroom.
_Avoid_: Business listing, building listing

**Review**:
One signed-in visitor's editable assessment of a restroom they have used, containing an overall rating and optional written feedback. It may also contain separate scores for cleanliness, supplies, and facility condition.
_Avoid_: Recommendation (when referring to user feedback)

**Recommendation**:
A restroom suggested by Poopable, prioritizing proximity, the seeker's access needs, and expected opening status, then considering ratings and recent usability reports. Permanently closed facilities and facilities with unknown attributes required by the seeker are ineligible; unrated facilities remain eligible.
_Avoid_: Review (when referring to the app's suggestion)

**Rating**:
A score from 1 to 5 expressing a reviewer's assessment of the overall restroom experience. Optional dimension ratings assess cleanliness, supplies, and facility condition separately.

**Overall score**:
The arithmetic mean of a restroom's current overall ratings, displayed with its review count. An edited review replaces its previous rating; optional dimension ratings remain separate.
_Avoid_: Rating (when referring to the aggregate)

**Search location**:
The point around which someone searches for restrooms, either their current location or a location in an area they choose.

**Nearby**:
Within the chosen search radius of the search location, initially 1 km and expandable. Distance means direct distance, distinct from walking distance.

**Access conditions**:
Requirements or restrictions on using a restroom, such as customer-only access, entrance fees, and opening hours.

**Restroom submission**:
A proposed new restroom listing contributed by a signed-in user and awaiting review before becoming publicly visible.
_Avoid_: Published listing (when the submission is still awaiting review)

**Expected open**:
A restroom whose opening hours indicate it should currently be open. Its present usability may still be unknown.

**Confirmed usable**:
A restroom reported usable by a signed-in visitor at a stated time. This observation is distinct from expected opening hours and does not guarantee present usability.

**Usability report**:
A signed-in visitor's timestamped observation that a restroom is usable, closed, or out of service.
_Avoid_: Review, live guarantee

**Recent usability report**:
A usability report observed within the past two hours. When recent reports conflict, the latest observation determines the displayed reported condition; older observations remain history.

**Unknown usability**:
The absence of a recent usability report for a restroom, even if its hours indicate it is expected to be open.

**Access needs**:
A seeker's requirements concerning wheelchair access, gender designation or an all-gender option, and baby-changing facilities. Each listing attribute can be unknown; unknown does not mean absent.

**Guest**:
A person using Poopable without signing in, who can find restrooms, read reviews, and receive recommendations. A guest can search a chosen area without sharing their current location.

**Moderator**:
A person responsible for approving new restroom submissions and listing corrections before publication, initially the Poopable owner. Reviews and usability reports appear immediately and can be reported for moderation.

**Listing correction**:
A proposed change to an existing restroom listing, requiring moderator approval before changing its public information.

**Unrated restroom**:
A restroom with no ratings, shown as "No ratings yet." The absence of ratings is distinct from a poor rating.

**Permanently closed restroom**:
A restroom no longer available on an ongoing basis, whose listing and history are retained but which is excluded from recommendations. This differs from a visitor's temporary closed observation.

**Closed**:
A reported condition in which a restroom is not open for use at the time of observation.

**Out of service**:
A reported condition in which a restroom cannot be used because its facilities are not functioning.
